### Changed

- Names, paths and commands in task texts, hints, solutions and answers of the assistant stand out:
  inline code is a chip with its own colour in both themes. A chip used to differ from its card by
  a contrast of 1.05:1 in the dark theme and had the colour of the text around it. In a table a
  short command such as `type -t` stays on one line.
- The dark theme is easier on the eyes: text a little below white on surfaces a little above black,
  instead of near-white on near-black. Every pair of text and surface colours is measured in a test
  and reaches 4.5:1.
- The footer (version, source, "Created by Mistral N") stays in view in practice and during an
  exam, as one compact line below the workspace.
