# Credits for the tour video

`kubedojo-promo.mp4` shows kubedojo itself, recorded key by key from a local run: the practice
workspace, the assistant (answering on a local model), a track, a lesson, an exam simulation with its
report and the home page. The pictures of the learning loop, of one machine and of a team's cluster
are diagrams drawn after the ones in the README; the Grafana panel is `docs/images/grafana.png`.

## Music

"Lofi Hip Hop Upbeat" by raspberrymusic, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raspberrymusic_-_Lofi_Hip_Hop_Upbeat.ogg>.
The video uses the track from 0:39 to its end, without two bars of its quiet middle part, and at a
lower volume.

## Sound effects

Typing, key press, click, whoosh, pop and the bass impact come from the sound library bundled with
the [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com) command line tool, which also rendered the video.
