# Credits for the tour video

`kubedojo-promo.mp4` shows kubedojo itself, recorded from a local run.

## Music

"Lofi Hip Hop Upbeat" by raspberrymusic, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raspberrymusic_-_Lofi_Hip_Hop_Upbeat.ogg>.
The track was shortened to the length of the video (it starts at its second bar), lowered in
volume and faded out.

## Sound effects

Typing, click, whoosh, chime and pop come from the sound library bundled with the
[HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com) command line tool, which also rendered the video.
